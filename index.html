Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Roll Arena X, developed and owned by MAHESH SHASHIKANT NANDRE ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application ("Application" or "Game").

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a functional and enjoyable gaming experience, we collect and process certain categories of information:

  • Authentication Data: If you choose to log in using Facebook Login, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture to establish your user account profile and sync progress across devices.
  • Device Information: We collect hardware specifications, OS version, unique device identifiers, and Crashlytics diagnostics to troubleshoot errors and optimize performance.
  • Gameplay & Transaction Progress: We store virtual currency balances (Gems, Coins) and unlocked cosmetics (Trails, Frames) locally and on secure Firebase backend systems to prevent progress loss.

2. Third-Party Integrations

Our Application integrates trusted third-party SDKs to enable core features. These partners may collect identifiers directly from your device:

  • Facebook SDK: Enables secure profile login and friend-matchmaking features.
  • Google Play Billing API: Processes secure in-app purchases for Coins and Gems. We do not store or process payment card details.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymous usage trends and crash reports to improve application stability.

3. Data Retention and Protection

We implement security protocols to protect your credentials. We retain your login and gameplay progress only for as long as your account remains active. You can request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Facebook Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

Roll Arena X uses Facebook Login to authenticate users. In compliance with Meta's developer policies, we provide a data deletion callback implementation and instruction workflow for users to request data removal.

If you wish to delete your activity or account data associated with Roll Arena X, you can do so by following these simple steps:

Method 1: Request Deletion via Facebook Settings

  1. Log into your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Scroll down and select Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Roll Arena X in the list and click the Remove button.
  4. A confirmation dialog will appear. Check the box to "Delete posts, videos or events Roll Arena X posted on your timeline" if desired, then click Remove.
  5. Under the removed apps list, you can click on Roll Arena X to view details and click Send Request to trigger our automatic deletion callback.

Method 2: Direct Email Deletion Request

If you prefer to have your account and gameplay data deleted manually from our servers:

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to: rollarenax@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Roll Arena X - Account Deletion Request.
  3. Provide your Game Profile ID or the email associated with your Facebook Account.

Once received, we will verify your request and completely delete all your profile credentials, cosmetic purchases, and coin records from our databases within 48 hours, sending you a confirmation email when complete.
